# Project brief — example applicant

## The question

How the schedule of repeated exposure, massed or spaced and in which order,
changes what people later recognise, and whether a model that learns from
sequences of experience predicts that change in visual cortex as well as in
behaviour.

## What I would do

Fit a sequence-learning model to recognition data from exposure schedules I
have already run, then test what it predicts about cortical responses with
fMRI.

## What I would need from a mentor

- Models of learning from sequences of experience that predict cortical
  responses, not only behaviour.
- Human fMRI of repeated exposure, which I have not run myself.
